FAQ

Is Padded a real booking service?

No — this is a class mini-project built to practice HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SEO basics, Git, and deployment. Listings are illustrative examples.

Do you charge finder's fees?

In concept, no. Padded is meant to connect renters and landlords directly without a commission.

How current are the listings?

In a real version, listings would refresh weekly. For this project, the dataset is fixed sample data.
