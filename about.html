About the project

Built by a student,
for students hunting a pad.

Every semester, new FEU students spend their first week walking up and down España asking sari-sari store owners if they know a room for rent. Padded puts that same information in one place.

Why España Blvd?

España Boulevard and the Morayta strip near LRT-2 and Lacson Avenue is one of the densest student housing strips in Manila — FEU, UST, and several other schools all draw from the same handful of side streets: P. Noval, Morayta, Dagohoy, and Dapitan.

Prices and vacancies change fast here, and most listings never make it further than a printed tarpaulin taped to a gate. Padded exists to put that same information online, sorted and searchable.

  • 📍
    Walk-time first. Every listing shows real minutes from FEU Main Gate, not vague "near campus" claims.
  • 💸
    No agent fees. You contact the lister directly through this site — Padded doesn't charge a booking commission.
  • 🔍
    Built for filtering. Sort by budget, room type, and gender preference in a couple of clicks.

A note on the data

All listings on this site are fictional examples created for a class assignment on HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SEO, Git, and deployment. Names, prices, and photos (simple illustrations) are placeholders standing in for what a real listings dataset would look like.
